# Narration script — Before You Paste It Into AI

*This is the narration text corresponding to the supplied recording, with punctuation and paragraph breaks for readability. The recording contains an unintended opening “A” sound; that sound was trimmed for the edited video.*

AI can save time at work. But before you paste information into a tool, there's something worth checking.

A document might contain personal details, confidential information, or material that shouldn't leave your organisation.

Use a simple three-step check: **Pause. Check. Decide.**

First, pause. Don't automatically paste everything into the chat. Take a moment to consider what you're about to share.

Next, check. Does the information contain personal, confidential or non-public details? And is this AI tool approved for the information you're handling?

Finally, decide. You may be able to remove sensitive details or rephrase your request. If the tool or information isn't approved, don't paste it.

The safest choice isn't always to paste. Sometimes it's to edit, use another approved method, or stop.
