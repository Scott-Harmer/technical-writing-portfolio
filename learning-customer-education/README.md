# Learning & Customer Education

## Project overview

A collection of learning design, customer education and instructional design portfolio samples. The work includes a needs-led learning programme, retrospective analyses using instructional design frameworks, Articulate Rise 360 e-learning samples and selected original Cambridge DELTA evidence.

Together, the projects demonstrate how diagnostic evidence, learner needs, performance considerations and assessment choices can inform the structure of a learning experience.

## Learning programme design

A ten-week learning pathway developed from diagnostic evidence and identified learning needs. The challenge was to turn findings about learners into coherent priorities, a practical programme sequence and opportunities to evaluate progress.

**Skills demonstrated:** Needs analysis, learning objectives, programme structure, learner-centred planning and evaluation.

- [View the Learning Programme Design (PDF)](learning-programme-design.pdf)

## ADDIE in practice

A **retrospective analysis** of the same learning programme through the ADDIE framework: Analyse, Design, Develop, Implement and Evaluate.

The sample demonstrates the ability to examine an existing programme systematically, explain design decisions and identify how the phases relate to one another. It does not claim that the original programme was developed by following ADDIE as a formal process.

- [Read ADDIE in Practice (PDF)](addie-in-practice.pdf)

## Applying an Action Mapping lens

A **retrospective, performance-focused analysis** of the programme using Action Mapping concepts.

This sample considers the relationship between learning activities and the behaviours or performance outcomes they are intended to support.

- [Read the Action Mapping analysis (PDF)](action-mapping-lens.pdf)

## Articulate Rise 360 e-learning

A separate set of e-learning samples covering AI-related judgement, effective one-to-one meetings and DISC. The samples demonstrate instructional content development, scenario-based approaches and assessment design, with attention to accessibility and delivery.

The Rise 360 samples were tested as SCORM packages in TalentLMS. The linked PDFs offer a convenient portfolio viewing format.

**Skills demonstrated:** E-learning development, instructional sequencing, scenario design, assessment, accessibility awareness, SCORM publishing and LMS testing.
