# Industrial Pump Maintenance Documentation

**Technical Writing & Editing Portfolio Project | Work in Progress**

## Project Overview

This project presents two technical documentation samples developed around the fictional SIP 80-200 close-coupled centrifugal water pump.

The documents demonstrate approaches to technical writing, editorial restructuring, maintenance procedure development, safety communication, and document quality control.

Both samples are presented as working documents to illustrate the technical documentation development process rather than as final, approved maintenance instructions.

## Portfolio Documents

### 1. Industrial Pump Maintenance Manual — Technical Editing & Documentation

[View maintenance manual PDF](Industrial_Pump_Maintenance_Manual_Technical_Editing_Portfolio.pdf)

A 14-page maintenance manual demonstrating the organisation and presentation of technical information for industrial equipment.

**Key features:**
- Structured maintenance and inspection procedures
- Safety messages using DANGER, WARNING, CAUTION, NOTICE, and NOTE classifications
- Equipment specifications and component identification
- Illustrated parts information and maintenance tables
- Consistent headings, procedural steps, and document navigation

**Document status:** Working draft. Further layout refinement, editorial quality assurance, and technical validation are required.

### 2. Industrial Pump Maintenance Manual — EU Technical Editorial Reconstruction

[View EU editorial reconstruction PDF](Industrial_Pump_Maintenance_Manual_EU_Editorial_Reconstruction.pdf)

A 14-page editorial reconstruction demonstrating a more controlled approach to technical documentation intended for an EU publication context.

**Key features:**
- Document identification, revision status, and approval controls
- Structured maintenance procedures with defined personnel responsibilities
- Safety information and equipment-specific warnings
- Engineering HOLD notices identifying information requiring verification
- Provisional technical values clearly distinguished from approved specifications
- EU release and technical validation register
- Editorial sign-off and publication requirements

**Document status:** Editorial master — not released. Engineering validation, regulatory review, and publication approval remain outstanding.

## Technical Documentation Skills Demonstrated

- Technical writing and editing
- Information architecture and document restructuring
- Procedural writing and task sequencing
- Safety and warning communication
- Technical terminology and consistency
- Tables, figures, and component references
- Document control and revision management
- Editorial quality assurance
- Identification and documentation of technical information gaps
- Preparation of documentation for engineering and compliance review

## Editorial Approach

The project explores two complementary aspects of technical documentation: creating a readable maintenance manual and developing a controlled editorial version suitable for further technical review.

The EU editorial reconstruction makes unresolved technical information explicit through HOLD notices and a validation register. This demonstrates the distinction between editorial preparation and engineering approval.

## Project Status and Disclaimer

**This is a fictional technical documentation portfolio exercise.**

The manufacturer, pump model, and associated technical data are presented for demonstration purposes.

Neither document has been approved for operational use. Technical specifications, maintenance limits, safety procedures, and regulatory requirements must be verified by appropriately qualified personnel before publication or use on actual equipment.

The documents are included to demonstrate technical documentation and editing skills, not to provide instructions for maintaining real machinery.
