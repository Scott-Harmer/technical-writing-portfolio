# Ukraine — The Education Industry: Strategic Analysis and Market Outlook (2021)

A 45-page strategic research report prepared for a private client considering investment in international education in Kyiv, Ukraine.

The report combines a decision-focused client report with a detailed research annex. It examines demand, competition, regulation and accreditation, school operating models, financial feasibility, implementation risks, and a proposed investment roadmap. Its evidence includes comparative tables, financial projections, risk analysis, source research, and an exploratory survey of 23 Kyiv parents, with methodological limitations identified.

## Skills demonstrated

Research and analysis, executive and business report writing, information architecture, evidence-based recommendations, financial data presentation, and risk communication.

## Historical context

Completed in October 2021, before Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Findings, assumptions, and projections reflect information available at that time and should not be interpreted as a current assessment of the Ukrainian education market. The report did consider Russia-related escalation among its risks.
