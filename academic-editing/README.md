# Academic Editing: Doctoral Thesis

An anonymised academic editing sample drawn from an ongoing, four-year collaboration with a doctoral researcher writing a three-paper thesis on corporate governance and boards in Russian private companies.

The project demonstrates substantive academic editing, improvements to clarity and cohesion, terminology consistency, APA 7 citation checks, and constructive queries to the author. The aim is to strengthen doctoral-level English while preserving the author's meaning, academic voice and ownership of the research.

The three-paragraph sample was developed collaboratively for portfolio demonstration. Outstanding source queries remain visible rather than being presented as resolved.
