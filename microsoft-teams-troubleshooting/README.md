# Microsoft Teams: Microphone and Camera Troubleshooting

## Project overview

An illustrated, seven-page troubleshooting guide designed to help employees resolve common Microsoft Teams microphone and camera problems on Windows 11 and macOS before contacting IT support.

The guide provides task-focused instructions for checking Teams controls, selecting the correct devices and reviewing operating-system permissions. A separate one-page case study explains the documentation approach.

## The documentation challenge

Microphone and camera problems can have several possible causes, including application settings, device selection and operating-system permissions.

The challenge was to present these checks in a sequence that employees without specialist IT knowledge could follow, while accommodating differences between Windows 11 and macOS.

The instructions also needed to be concise enough for use during a technical problem, when readers may be under time pressure or experiencing frustration.

## My approach

- **Task-focused information architecture:** Organised troubleshooting around actions users can take to identify and resolve common problems.
- **Procedural writing:** Developed clear, sequential instructions with an emphasis on observable user actions.
- **Cross-platform documentation:** Addressed relevant differences between Windows 11 and macOS.
- **Visual communication:** Used annotated screenshots to help readers locate settings and controls.
- **Plain language:** Applied concise, consistent wording to reduce the effort required to follow instructions.
- **Quality review:** Considered clarity, consistency and usability across the guide.

## Technical writing principles

The writing draws on principles associated with ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, particularly clarity, consistency and controlled sentence structure.

This is an application of selected writing principles, **not a claim of formal ASD-STE100 compliance**.

## Skills demonstrated

Procedural writing, troubleshooting workflow design, information architecture, cross-platform documentation, screenshot annotation, visual communication, plain language and technical editing.
