# Microsoft Teams Microphone and Camera Troubleshooting

An illustrated, seven-page troubleshooting guide designed to help employees resolve common Microsoft Teams microphone and camera problems on Windows 11 and macOS before contacting IT support.

## Deliverables

- **[Troubleshooting guide (PDF)](teams-troubleshooting-guide.pdf):** Task-focused instructions for checking Teams controls, device selection, and operating-system permissions, supported by annotated visuals.
- **[Project case study (PDF)](teams-project-case-study.pdf):** A one-page account of the audience, information architecture, writing decisions, visual design, and quality review.

## Skills demonstrated

Procedural writing, troubleshooting workflows, cross-platform documentation, screenshot annotation, information design, and user-focused editing. The writing draws on principles of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English; no claim of formal compliance is made.
