# Technical Writing & Learning Development Portfolio

I enjoy solving communication problems: understanding complex information, identifying what people need to know, and finding the clearest way to explain it.

My approach combines analytical thinking, structured communication, problem-solving and an understanding of how people learn. I've developed these skills through technical documentation review, commercial editing, B2B client engagement and more than 25 years in education and training.

My experience includes working directly with business clients to identify requirements, develop tailored solutions and coordinate their delivery. I've also designed and standardised learning programmes, led academic operations across multiple locations and collaborated with specialists to improve information quality.

Formal qualifications in technical communication, structured authoring and instructional practice complement this experience.

I bring a systematic, practical and audience-focused approach to my work. Whether I'm reviewing technical documentation, developing instructions or designing learning materials, my aim is to produce information that meets user needs while supporting business objectives.

**This portfolio brings together two complementary areas of professional expertise: Technical Writing & Documentation and Learning & Development.**

### 7. Ukraine Education Industry Report — Research & Strategic Communication

A 45-page strategic report prepared for a private client evaluating investment opportunities in international education in Kyiv.

The work integrates market and regulatory research, competitor comparisons, financial projections, risk analysis and an exploratory survey of 23 parents into a decision-focused report with a supporting research annex.

The project demonstrates the synthesis of complex evidence, communication of uncertainty and presentation of findings to support strategic decision-making.

The report was also converted into structured DITA/XML and DocBook 5.0 documentation, demonstrating XML authoring, publication structure, schema validation and HTML5 publishing.

The DITA documentation was built using DITA Open Toolkit 4.4.1 with validation enabled, and the DocBook XML passed formal RELAX NG schema validation using `xmllint`.

**Skills:** Research synthesis, strategic reporting, financial information design, risk communication, evidence-based recommendations, DITA/XML, DocBook 5, structured authoring, schema validation, HTML5 publishing.

### 4. AI-Assisted Video Production & Microlearning

**Before You Paste It Into AI**

A complete multimedia production project demonstrating the development of a workplace AI-safety microlearning experience.

The project combines instructional design, video production, AI-generated narration, avatar integration, multimedia editing and quality assurance.

Its central learning message introduces a practical three-step check before sharing workplace information with an AI tool: **Pause → Check → Decide**.

**Skills:** Video production, instructional design, microlearning, scriptwriting, AI-generated narration, speaking-avatar production, multimedia editing, quality assurance.
